What happens next

Match your columns, check extracted leads, then confirm the import. Duplicate email addresses are skipped; existing leads are preserved.

Files are parsed locally, with no AI charge. Excel uses the first worksheet; Word uses the first table. PDFs need selectable text arranged in columns or delimited rows. Scans, images, and free-form documents need conversion to a lead table first.

Temporary extracted rows expire after 24 hours and are cleared on your next upload. The original file is not saved.
